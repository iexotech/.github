# Security policy

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately. Don't open a public issue.

Send a direct message to [@iexotech on X](https://x.com/iexotech) asking for a private channel, and don't include details in the first message. A dedicated security address is coming.

Include the affected repo or URL, steps to reproduce and the potential impact. We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days and will keep you updated until it's resolved.
